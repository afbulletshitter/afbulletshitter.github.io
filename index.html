ALQ writing guide

A scanner-friendly quick reference for writing performance statements with Airman Leadership Qualities.

Performance statement basics

Use a standalone sentence in plain language. State the action and at least one impact or result. Keep it concise, avoid uncommon acronyms, and write so someone outside the career field can understand it.

Executing the Mission

Job Proficiency
Demonstrates knowledge and professional skill to produce quality mission results.
Initiative
Acts independently, identifies needs, and takes appropriate action without waiting to be told.
Adaptability
Adjusts effectively to changing conditions, priorities, information, and obstacles.

Leading People

Inclusion & Teamwork
Works across differences, builds trust, and contributes to a team where people can succeed.
Emotional Intelligence
Recognizes and manages emotions while responding constructively to others.
Communication
Shares information clearly, listens effectively, and tailors the message to the audience.

Managing Resources

Stewardship
Uses people, time, funds, equipment, and facilities responsibly to accomplish the mission.
Accountability
Takes ownership of actions, decisions, standards, and results.

Improving the Unit

Decision Making
Uses available information, judgment, and risk assessment to choose effective action.
Innovation
Finds and applies better ways to solve problems, improve processes, and increase effectiveness.
